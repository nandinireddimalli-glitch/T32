# 5. Content Pillars

Promotional content is kept at about 20% so the feed gives value first and sells second.

| Pillar | Theme | Share of calendar | What it includes | Why it matters | Example posts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Educational | Learn by Building | 22% | Tutorials, project ideas, roadmaps, tool explainers, code tips | Builds authority and earns saves/shares; attracts students searching for help | 5 beginner AI projects you can finish in a weekend; Deploy a site in 60 seconds; README writing guide |
| Informational | Career Compass | 22% | Job-market insights, role explainers, resume and interview advice, alumni stories, FAQs | Answers career anxiety; builds trust with switchers and parents | 6 entry-level tech roles and the skills each needs; Day in the life of a junior developer; Honest answers for parents |
| Engagement | Builder Community | 35% | Polls, quizzes, memes, challenges, user-generated content, live sessions | Boosts algorithmic reach, builds community and generates UGC | #ProjectNotPromise challenge; 'Guess the output' code quizzes; Relatable developer memes |
| Promotional | Inside Vidyut | 20% | Workshop and cohort announcements, testimonials, offers, deadlines | Converts warm audience into leads and enrolments — capped at ~20% so the feed never feels like an ad | Free workshop announcement; Jan 2027 cohort: what you'll build; Early-bird closing reminder |
