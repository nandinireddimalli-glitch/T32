# 9. Content Formats

| Format | Specs | Best used for | Best practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reel | 9:16, 1080×1920, 15–45 s | Reach and discovery; tutorials, memes, recaps | Hook in first 2 seconds, on-screen captions, trending audio |
| Instagram carousel | 4:5, 1080×1350, 6–10 slides | Saves; step-by-step education, lists | Bold cover slide, one idea per slide, CTA on last slide |
| Instagram Stories | 9:16, 1080×1920 | Daily touchpoint; polls, quizzes, countdowns, Q&A | Use interactive stickers, link sticker for registrations |
| Instagram / LinkedIn Live | Live video, 30–60 min | Workshop coverage, AMAs with mentors | Announce 48 h before; pin a comment with the link |
| LinkedIn text post | 150–250 words | Insights, stories, opinions | Strong first line, short paragraphs, end with a question |
| LinkedIn document carousel | PDF, 1080×1350, 8–12 pages | Roadmaps, checklists, syllabus | Value-first; brand only on first and last page |
| LinkedIn poll | 2–4 options, 1 week | Audience research and engagement | Share results in a follow-up post |
| Blog article | 1,200–1,800 words | SEO traffic and trust | Target one keyword, add FAQs, internal links, CTA box |
| Landing page | Single page | Conversions (workshop, challenge, cohort) | One CTA, social proof, short form, UTM tracking |
| YouTube Short | 9:16, under 60 s | Extra reach from repurposed Reels | Re-edit captions; add subscribe CTA |
| User-generated content (UGC) | Reposts with credit | Social proof and community | Always ask permission and tag the creator |
| WhatsApp broadcast | Short text + image/video | Lead nurturing and reminders | Max 2–3 per week to avoid fatigue |
