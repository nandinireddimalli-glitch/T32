# 4. Competitor Analysis

> Competitors are grouped by type. Observations are general, qualitative market patterns — not detailed claims about any single company.

| Competitor type | Examples | Strengths | Weaknesses | Their content | Gap for Vidyut Labs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Global MOOC platforms | e.g. Coursera, Udemy | Huge course catalogue, strong brand recognition, low price per course | Mostly self-paced; little personal mentorship or local placement help; many learners don't finish | Course ads, discount promotions, generic career content | Accountability and local, mentor-led project feedback |
| Large Indian upskilling companies | e.g. upGrad, Scaler | Big brands, structured programs, large marketing budgets, strong outcome storytelling | Premium pricing; programmes often aimed at working professionals; sales-led enrolment process | Learner success stories, webinars, performance ads, LinkedIn thought leadership | Affordable option for tier-2/3 students with a friendlier, community-first tone |
| Offline training institutes | e.g. Ameerpet-area coaching centres | Low fees, local presence, strong word of mouth, Telugu-friendly | Large batches, syllabus can lag industry, weak social media and branding | Banner ads, pamphlets, basic Facebook posts | Modern stacks (AI, cloud), deployed projects and a credible online presence |
| Free YouTube creators | e.g. Telugu and English coding channels | Free, highly trusted, regional-language content, entertaining | No structure, feedback, deadlines or placement support | Long tutorials, Shorts, live streams | Turning watchers into builders through deadlines, reviews and a cohort |
