# 3. Marketing Objectives (SMART)

All objectives are **S**pecific, **M**easurable, **A**chievable, **R**elevant and **T**ime-bound (1–30 Dec 2026).

| # | Goal | SMART objective | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Awareness | Grow Instagram followers from 1,200 to 3,000 and LinkedIn page followers from 500 to 1,200 by 30 Dec 2026. | Follower count (platform insights) |
| 2 | Engagement | Reach an average engagement rate of ≥5% on Instagram and ≥3% on LinkedIn across all posts in the 30 days. | Engagement rate per post |
| 3 | Traffic | Drive 8,000 website sessions, with at least 40% from social and 20% from organic search. | GA4 sessions by source (UTM-tagged) |
| 4 | Lead generation | Get 600 registrations for the free 'Build Your First AI App' workshop (12 Dec) and 150 counselling calls booked. | Form submissions, booked calls |
| 5 | Conversion | Enrol 40 paid learners into the January 2027 cohort at a cost per acquisition of ≤₹3,750. | Enrolments, CAC |
| 6 | Community | Receive 150 #ProjectNotPromise challenge submissions and grow the WhatsApp community to 1,000 members. | Submissions, community size |
