## Hashtag rules

- Instagram: 3–5 hashtags per post — 1–2 branded + 2–3 niche/local. Fewer, more relevant hashtags work better than long lists.
- LinkedIn: 3 hashtags per post — 1 branded + 2 professional (#TechCareers, #FullStackDeveloper).
- Every campaign gets one branded hashtag so its posts and user content can be tracked.
- Put keywords in captions and on-screen text too — Instagram and LinkedIn search read captions, not just hashtags.
- Review hashtag performance every week and swap out tags that bring no reach.
