## Measurement plan

- Tag every link with UTM parameters: utm_source=instagram|linkedin|whatsapp, utm_medium=organic|paid, utm_campaign=ai-workshop|projectnotpromise|newyear-stack.
- Track all numbers in the 'KPI Tracker' sheet every Monday; compare actual vs target.
- Weekly 30-minute review: double down on the top 3 posts' formats, drop the bottom 3.
- After Day 14, adjust posting times based on real Instagram/LinkedIn audience data.
- End-of-month report: what worked, what didn't, learnings for the January cohort.
