The business

Vidyut Labs is a fictional Hyderabad tech school used for this project. It runs 12-week, project-based cohorts in AI/ML and full-stack development.

Turn engineering students and early-career professionals from tier-2/3 colleges into job-ready builders through real projects, not certificates.

  • 12-week project-based cohorts in AI/ML Engineering and Full-Stack Web Development
  • Free weekend workshops (e.g. 'Build Your First AI App')
  • 1:1 mentor code reviews by working engineers
  • Placement support: resume, GitHub and mock interviews
Location
Madhapur, Hyderabad (online + weekend offline labs)
Pricing
₹29,999 per cohort (EMI and merit scholarships available)
What makes it different
Every learner ships 4 deployed projects with mentor code review, with doubt-clearing in English and Telugu.
Starting point
Vidyut Labs is 1 year old, has ~1,200 Instagram followers, ~500 LinkedIn page followers and a basic website. Most enquiries come through word of mouth. The next cohort starts in January 2027.
For engineering students and early-career switchers in Telugu states who are tired of collecting certificates, Vidyut Labs is the project-first tech school that gets you to ship 4 deployed projects with mentor code reviews — unlike self-paced platforms or crowded institutes, we hold you accountable until your portfolio is interview-ready.

Demographics

  • Age 18–30 (decision makers), 40–55 (parents who pay)
  • Education: B.Tech/BCA/B.Sc (CS, IT, AI, ECE) — many from non-premier colleges
  • Income: students depend on family; switchers earn roughly ₹2.5–5 LPA
  • Languages: English + Telugu (Hindi as a third language)

Psychographics

  • Ambitious and career-anxious; compare themselves with peers on LinkedIn
  • Value practical, 'show me' learning over lectures
  • Distrust institutes that promise '100% placement'
  • Price-sensitive but will pay for clear outcomes

Pain points

  • College curriculum is theory-heavy; no portfolio to show recruiters
  • Overwhelmed by too many free tutorials with no structure or feedback
  • Resumes get rejected without interviews
  • Fear of being 'too late' (switchers) or 'not smart enough' (students)

Online behaviour

  • Instagram: Reels and carousels daily, mostly 7–11 PM
  • YouTube: long tutorials and Telugu tech channels
  • LinkedIn: job search, following companies, reading career posts
  • WhatsApp/Telegram: college groups, job-alert channels
  • Google: searches like 'full stack course in Hyderabad', 'AI projects for final year'

Buying triggers

  • Semester breaks and results announcements
  • Campus placement season and internship drives
  • New Year resolutions ('2027 is my year')
  • Seeing a peer or senior get placed

Campaigns

Four campaigns, one per week, each moving people a step further: awareness, leads, community, enrolment.

  1. Week 1 (Day 1–7) · Awareness

    Projects > Certificates

    A myth-busting series showing that recruiters look at what you've built, not how many certificates you have. Sets up the brand's core belief.

    Assets: Opening Reel, LinkedIn document carousel, SEO blog, Story polls

    Target: Reach 60,000 accounts; +500 Instagram followers

  2. Week 2 (Day 5–14, workshop on Sat 12 Dec) · Lead generation

    Build Your First AI App (free workshop)

    A free 3-hour online + offline workshop where beginners build and deploy a simple AI app. Registration collects leads; attendees get a follow-up counselling call.

    Assets: Announcement Reel, event page, countdown Stories, WhatsApp reminders, live coverage, recap Reel

    Target: 600 registrations, 50% attendance, 150 counselling calls

  3. Week 3 (Day 15–22) · Engagement + UGC

    #ProjectNotPromise Challenge

    Build anything in 7 days, post it with #ProjectNotPromise and tag @vidyutlabs. Top 3 win 50% / 25% / 25% scholarships; community votes for a 'People's Choice'.

    Assets: Launch Reel, rules landing page, idea carousel, UGC reposts, voting Stories, winners Reel

    Target: 150 submissions; 400 hashtag uses; engagement rate ≥6%

  4. Week 4 (Day 22–30) · Conversion

    New Year, New Stack

    Enrolment push for the January 2027 cohort with an early-bird fee ending 30 Dec. Uses alumni stories, a transparent fee/EMI explainer and a Telugu parent open house to remove objections.

    Assets: Cohort carousel, syllabus document, parent open house, countdown Stories, retargeting ads

    Target: 40 enrolments; CAC ≤ ₹3,750
